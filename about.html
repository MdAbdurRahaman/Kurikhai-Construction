Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Singapore Construction & Renovation

Founded in Singapore, Tabeeb Contractor Pte Ltd is built on a foundation of safety compliance, stellar engineering, and premium finish quality.

We service residential estates (HDB, condominiums, landed homes), commercial office hubs, retail spaces, and industrial facilities across Singapore. Our team features BCA-certified supervisors, licensed builders, certified plumbers, registered electricians, and waterproofing specialists. By combining multi-disciplinary expertise under one roof, we eliminate coordination delays, control costs, and guarantee timely handovers.

From commercial reinstatement, structural hacking, and cement screed flooring to PU injection waterproofing and bespoke alterations, we guide our clients through initial design drawings, authority permit submissions, and final inspection sign-offs.

How We Work

Our Project Lifecycle

We follow a structured, transparent process to take your project from blueprint to reality without any hidden costs.

01

Consultation

We assess your property floor plans, discuss budget parameters, and identify service needs (demolition, plumbing, structural extension).

02

Approvals & Plans

We compile architectural drafts. Home extensions and alterations are submitted for relevant authority permits (BCA/URA approvals).

03

Site Execution

Our skilled tradesmen carry out hacking, MEP routing, flooring, false ceiling, painting, and waterproofing works under expert supervision.

04

Handover

A rigorous quality inspection check is run. We certify waterproofing leak tests, test electrical loads, and hand over a clean property.
