Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Quality Construction

Founded in Singapore, Kurikhai Construction Pte Ltd is built on a foundation of safety compliance, stellar engineering, and premium finish quality.

We service residential estates, commercial office hubs, and industrial sites. Our team features BCA-certified supervisors, certified plumbers, registered electricians, and waterproofing experts. By combining multi-disciplinary expertise under one roof, we eliminate coordination issues, control costs, and deliver projects on schedule.

From simple partitions, painting, and homogeneous tile flooring to major waterproofing projects and complex structural extensions, we guide our clients through design drawings, approval submissions, and final inspection handovers.

How We Work

Our Project Lifecycle

We follow a structured, transparent process to take your project from blueprint to reality without any surprise fees.

01

Consultation

We assess your property floor plans, discuss budget parameters, and identify service needs (demolition, plumbing, structural extension).

02

Approvals & Plans

We compile architectural drafts. Home extensions and alterations are submitted for relevant authority permits (BCA/URA approvals).

03

Site Execution

Our skilled tradesmen carry out hacking, MEP routing, flooring, false ceiling, painting, and waterproofing works under expert supervision.

04

Handover

A rigorous quality inspection check is run. We certify waterproofing leak tests, test electrical loads, and hand over a clean property.
