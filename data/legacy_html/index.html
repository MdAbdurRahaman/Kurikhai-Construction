Frequently Asked Questions

Key information about commercial reinstatement, direct contractor pricing, hacking permits, and renovation timelines in Singapore.

A direct contractor like Tabeeb Contractor employs their own workers and in-house trade specialists (hackers, tilers, electricians, plumbers). By engaging a direct contractor directly, you avoid the 20% to 35% commission markups typically added by interior design firms.

You enjoy direct site coordination, faster turnaround times, full transparency on material costs, and direct accountability with your dedicated project manager.

Commercial office reinstatement in Singapore typically ranges from S$5 to S$25 per sq ft, depending on size and lease specifications:

  • Small Offices (< 1,000 sq ft): S$5,000 to S$8,000 on average.
  • Medium to Large Offices (> 1,000 sq ft): S$8,000 to S$25,000+ depending on partition walls, ceiling tiles, and M&E termination.

Tabeeb Contractor provides free on-site surveys and an itemized quote guaranteed to pass your landlord's strict handover inspection.

Yes. For HDB properties, an official HDB Renovation Permit is legally required before starting any hacking of walls, floor tiles, or structural components. For commercial buildings, MCST and building management guidelines must be submitted with risk assessment and insurance.

Tabeeb Contractor assists with all necessary compliance applications, permit submissions, and protective site shielding to ensure smooth, legal execution.
